Hi, I'm Adriano

I'm a people, operations and project manager, and I put human talent at the centre of everything I do, together with technology.

My vision is a team where everyone feels important and everyone pulls in the same direction. A good manager doesn't know everything: they know which people to surround themselves with. I learn from my team, and I give them everything I know.

In 7+ years at Amazon I have worked with customers and vendors in support, then led onboarding for new hires, then moved into Learning & Development. I have run projects at local, national and international scale, always with a structured approach, a clear root cause and tangible results.

If you're building a team where people and technology grow together, I'd like to hear about it.
